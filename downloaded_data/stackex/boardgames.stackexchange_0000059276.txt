Q:

Why do some MTG cards specify "land and/or Gate"?

I've been toying with building a Maze's End Commander deck for a while now.
Digging around for cards for such a deck, I came across Navigation Orb and some other similar cards.
Why does these cards specify "and/or Gate" so often, when there are no Gate cards that aren't also Lands?

A:

If you read Navigation Orb, and probably most of those other cards, carefully, they actually specify "basic land and/or Gate", i.e. you can search for any Gate (which are indeed all lands), or any basic Island, Mountain, Forest, etc.
