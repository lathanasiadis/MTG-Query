Q:

Where can I play magic in Berlin?

I am in Berlin right now. Does anybody knows some good pub, where it is possible to play Magic: The Gathering?

A:

Well you could go to http://www.dailymtg.com and Enter Berlin into the Magic Locator app on the Left Side of the webpage.
That will tell you where all the game stores that sell Magic: The Gathering are and what events they are running.
