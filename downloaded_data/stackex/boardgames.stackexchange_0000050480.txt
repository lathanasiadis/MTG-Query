Q:

When will Arena's Workshop: Uncharted Paths be held?

In MTG Arena: State of the Game — February 2020, Wizards introduced a three-part workshop series: Power and Glory, Heroes of Theros, and Uncharted Paths.
I see on the Arena events calendar that Power and Glory is scheduled for February 24–26, followed by Heroes of Theros for March 2–4. However I can't see the date for Uncharted Paths. When will it be scheduled for, or has it already happened?

A:

The event is not featured on the Wizards calendar, but it is listed on the draftsim.com Arena schedule and will be March 9–11 2020. It follows on neatly in the schedule, but I don't know why it was missed on the official site.
