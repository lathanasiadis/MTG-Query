Q:

What are budget options for decks competitive in local standard tournaments?

With Wizards of the Coast pushing the format again, building budget-friendly decks to compete in the local scene becomes relevant. How can one approach the construction of budget decks for competitive local play of Standard (Friday Night Magic tournaments)?

A:

An attempt to write a somewhat timeless answer, rather than one which just happens to give an answer for Standard as of March 2024.

As much as possible, you want a mono-coloured deck. Basic lands are free, dual lands are not.
It's a somewhat timeless truth that aggro decks are cheap. Normally that means mono-red aggro/burn but can also mean something like white weenies or occasionally mono-black.
Look at MTG Goldfish's list of standard decks and see what's out there.

As of March 2024, this includes a mono-red aggro deck which comes in under $100 and has 5% of the metagame; some builds of the deck can be significantly cheaper. The disclaimer here is that cheap decks do tend to have an inflated metagame share precisely because they are cheap.
