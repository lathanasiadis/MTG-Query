Q:

Is there a way to tap Brokers Hideout for mana?

If I have a Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth in play can I tap a Brokers Hideout for one black mana before it gets sacrificed?

A:

Brokers Hideout is a triggered ability that goes on the stack. All players can react on that, and Brokers Hideout is a land on the battlefield at that moment, so with Urborg in play you can certainly get another mana out of it.
