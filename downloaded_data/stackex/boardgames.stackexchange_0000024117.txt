Q:

MTG Emrakul like cards

What are good cards like Emrakul, the Aeons Torn that are good for a green-black deck that revolves around Deathrender?

A:

If you are cheating creatures onto the battlefield (i.e. not paying their mana cost), the creatures themselves need not be in green/black/colorless.  Try searching for creatures with high converted mana costs.  They are the ones you are looking for to get the most value out of cards like deathrender.  Here are a few of my favorites:
Worldspine Wurm, Blightsteel Colossus, Iona, Shield of Emeria, Blazing Archon, Platinum Emperion, Avacyn, Angel of Hope
