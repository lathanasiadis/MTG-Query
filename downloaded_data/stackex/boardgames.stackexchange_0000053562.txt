Q:

Giving a 2/1 creature -2/-2

The card Disfigure reads:

Target creature gets -2/-2 until end of turn.

If I used that on Highland Game, a 2/1 creature, would Highland Game still die?

A:

Yes. A creature that has 0 toughness or less will die the next time any would would get priority.

State-Based Actions

704.5f If a creature has toughness 0 or less, it’s put into its owner’s graveyard. Regeneration can’t replace this event.
