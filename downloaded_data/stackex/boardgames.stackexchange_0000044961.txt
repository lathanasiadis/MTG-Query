Q:

Does Hour of Reckoning destroy enchantments?

Hour of Reckoning says

Destroy all nontoken creatures.

Does this mean all Enchantments will be destroyed as well?

A:

No, enchantments are unaffected by Hour of Reckoning, unless they're also creatures, like Nyx-Fleece Ram. (Here are a couple of other enchantment creatures.) 'Creature' and 'Enchantment' are different permanent types in Magic.
