Q:

With regards to an effect that triggers when a creature attacks, how does it entering the battlefield tapped and attacking apply?

Lets say I have Kaalia of the Vast on the battlefield. I attack with her, and cheat out Aurelia, the Warleader or Tyrant's Familiar. Would I get their "on attack" trigger? Or are they skipped. 

A:

No, you don't get the "on attack" trigger.
One of the rulings on Kaalia covers this point:

The creature card is already tapped and attacking as it’s put onto the battlefield. Any abilities that trigger when a creature becomes tapped or when a creature attacks won’t trigger for that card.
