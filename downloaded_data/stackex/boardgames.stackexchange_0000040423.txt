Q:

Simic Manipulator and polymorphist's jest combo

Once Polymorhist Jest is cast by an opponent during your attacking phase can a defending player also legally take full control over a target creature by tapping Simic Manipulator’s ability? 

A:

The only interaction between Polymorphic Jest and Simic Manipulator is that Polymorphic Jest can reduce a creature’s power, which reduces the number of counters you must remove from Simic in order to take control of it. So after Polymorphic Jest resolves; all the opponent’s creatures will be a 1/1 until end of turn, meaning that Simic can take control of one of them by removing just 1 counter.
