Q:

Nivmagus Elemental interactions with nivix cyclops, guttersnipe, and young pyromancer

if I exile a spell with Nivmagus Elemental, will the spell still cause the abilities of Nivix Cyclops, Guttersnipe and Young Pyromancer to trigger?

A:

Yes. All of your examples trigger when you cast a spell. They happen whether that spell resolves or not.
I have seen multiple EDH decks built specifically around cards such as those and Cipher, where the intent is to exile spells with Nivmagus whenever you don't really need them.
