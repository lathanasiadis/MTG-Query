Q:

When does Skullcage have its effect activated?

I am new to Magic, so forgive me if this is common knowledge.
Does the opponent's draw step happen before or after the Skullcage condition is checked?

A:

Skullcage triggers during your opponent's upkeep step, which is before their draw step. The order of steps in a Magic turn is

Untap
Upkeep
Draw
Precombat Main
Combat
Postcombat Main
End
