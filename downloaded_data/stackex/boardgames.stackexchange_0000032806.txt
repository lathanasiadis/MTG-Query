Q:

Sorceries in Combat phase

Can I cast sorceries in combat phase, or only instants? For example, could I cast Press into service after blockers are declared to take control of a declared blocker?

A:

You may only cast sorceries when it is your turn, during one of your main phases, while the stack is empty. So no you cannot cast them during combat.

307.1. A player who has priority may cast a sorcery card from his or her hand during a main phase of his or her turn when the stack is empty. Casting a sorcery as a spell uses the stack.
