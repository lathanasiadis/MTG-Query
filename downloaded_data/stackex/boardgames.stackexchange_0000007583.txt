Q:

What happens when you play Cloudshift on Borderland Ranger?

I believe that when I play Cloudshift which reads:
Exile target creature you control, then return that card to the battlefield under your control.
On Borderland Ranger which reads:
When Borderland Ranger enters the battlefield, you may search your library for a basic land card, reveal it, and put it into your hand. If you do, shuffle your library.
Then Borderland Ranger's triggered ability triggers. Is this correct?

A:

Yes, that's correct. Re-triggering "enters the battlefield" effects is certainly a good use of "flicker" cards like Cloudshift. Perhaps you'd be interested in this discussion of various uses of Cloudshift for more examples.
