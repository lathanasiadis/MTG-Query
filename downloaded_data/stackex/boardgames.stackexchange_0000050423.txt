Q:

Can I prevent a "You lose the game" event by countering the ability that creates it?

If I cast Chance for Glory, can I use Stifle, Tale's End, Disallow, or Voidslime to counter the game loss at the end of said extra turn?

A:

Chance for Glory's "At the beginning of that turn's end step, you lose the game" ability is a delayed trigger, so anything that counters triggered abilities will stop it. Hence yes, all four of the cards you mentioned will stop the game loss.
