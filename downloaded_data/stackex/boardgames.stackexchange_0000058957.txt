Q:

MTG dash and undying

Question about mtg rules.
When my opponent tries to kill Ragavan that I’ve cast for its dash cost and I protect it with Undying Malice.
Does Ragavan go back to my hand or does it stay on the battlefield with a +1/+1 counter?

A:

It will stay on the battlefield (after it has returned to the battlefield from Undying Malice).
The creature that is on the battlefield after Undying Malice triggers and resolves is not the same one that you cast for the Dash cost. Dash won’t do anything, because this particular Ragavan wasn’t cast for its Dash cost.
