Q:

Stack resolving with planeswalker and instants

I play Nissa, Voice of Zendikar a planeswalker. When it's in play my opponent plays Devour in Flames to deal 5 damage to my planeswalker. I want to use my effect "put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control" what will be resolve first? Does my creatures get +1/+1? 

A:

Nissa, Voice of Zendikar is a Planeswalker, which means that you can only activate her abilities on your turn, and Devour in Flames is a Sorcery, which means your opponent can only cast it on their turn. In other words, if your opponent is able to cast Devour in Flames, then you don't have a chance to activate Nissa's ability.
