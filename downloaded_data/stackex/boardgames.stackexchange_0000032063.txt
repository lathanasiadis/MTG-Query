Q:

MTG Does "Emerging" cause summoning sickness?

If I emerge a card like Mockery of Nature, does it get summoning sickness?

A:

Yes, your Mockery of Nature will have summoning sickness when it enters the battlefield.
When you pay the emerge cost of Mockery of Nature, it is just an alternate casting cost.  As such, it doesn't change the summoning sickness rule in anyway.
