Q:

Starfield Field of Nyx and Mirror March

If you cast a Mirror March and have five enchantments on the battlefield, including a Starfield of Nyx and another Mirror March, does the preexisting Mirror March copy the Mirror March that you just cast?

A:

When the Mirror March enters the battlefield, it copies itself and the existing Mirror March copies it.
The Starfield's effect applies to the Mirror March as soon as it is on the battlefield, so each Mirror March sees both itself and any later Mirror March enter the battlefield as a creature, triggering its ability.
