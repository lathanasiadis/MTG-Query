Changing decks in test of leadership in Arena?

I created a throw away deck, and entered it as a test of an idea into the current "Test of Leadership" in Arena.

A:

I figured out the problem.  I'd taken my crap deck, and modified it significantly.  And put in a legendary creature that was not part of Strixhaven.  Because of this, the UI didn't allow me to modify my deck.  After I removed the legendary creature, the deck appeared in the UI again.
