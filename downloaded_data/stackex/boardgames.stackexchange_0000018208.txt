Q:

You have to discard, but have no cards in your hand

If I play a spell that makes an opponent discard a card, but they have no cards in their hand, would they have to discard those cards from their library, or would nothing happen?

A:

Nothing happens.

He is instructed to discard a card.

701.7a To discard a card, move it from its owner’s hand to that player’s graveyard.

It's impossible for him to discard a card, so he does as much as possible.

609.3. If an effect attempts to do something impossible, it does only as much as possible.

It does not affect him.
