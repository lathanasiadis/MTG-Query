Q:

Is an Enchantment Creature card an Enchantment card?

is it possible to return an enchantment creature card (like 
Baleful Eidolon) with Dawn to Dusk from the graveyard? I'm not sure if an enchantment creature is a special type or just two types (enchantment and creature).
I appreciate your help!

A:

Yes, you can. Baleful Eidolon is an enchantment and a creature. Anything which targets or affects either of these permanent types can target or affect it.
