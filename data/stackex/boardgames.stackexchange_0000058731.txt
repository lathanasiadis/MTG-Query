Q:

What happens when two creatures are giving lifelink to my spells?

I play Firesong and Sunspeaker

Red instant and sorcery spells you control have lifelink.

and use Helm of the Host to make a copy of it. If I then use a spell like Lightning Bolt, will that trigger two separate instances of lifegain, meaning I gain 6 life rather than 3?

A:

The Comprehensive rules state that

702.15f Multiple instances of lifelink on the same object are redundant.

so, no: you'll gain only three life.
