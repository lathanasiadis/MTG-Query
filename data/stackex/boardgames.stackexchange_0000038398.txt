Q:

Is a creature considered to have died if it has negative toughness?

Ok so let's say my opponent has a Festering Mummy (It's a 1/1 and when it dies put a -1/-1/ counter on target creature) and I cast Splendid Agony (Distribute two -1/-1 counters among one or two target creatures) and I put a -1/-1 on the Festering Mummy, is it considered to have died and does its death effect still occur?

A:

Festering Mummy's triggered ability will trigger.
"To die" is synonymous with "to move from the battlefield to the graveyard" (for any reason). This includes when State-Based Actions move a creature to the graveyard for having negative toughness.
