Q:

Hammerfist Giant with Lifelink?

I have a Hammerfist Giant with a Felidar Umbra on it (enchantment aura that gives the creature Lifelink). There are 3 players and 7 non-flying creatures on the battlefield. I tap the giant to deal 4 damage to each player and non-flying creature. Do I gain 4 life, or do I gain 40 life?

A:

40 life.
Hammerfist Giant is dealing 40 total damage, so you gain 40 total life.
