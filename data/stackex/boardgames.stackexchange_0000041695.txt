Q:

Lion's Eye Diamond and madness

Can cards discarded to activate Lion's Eye Diamond be played for their madness cost with the mana produced by Lion's Eye Diamond?
For example:

I play Lion's Eye Diamond.
I have Alms of the Vein in my hand.
I crack Lion's Eye Diamond, discarding Alms of the Vein, getting {B}{B}{B}.
Can I play Alms of the Vein with the produced mana?

A:

Yes. Lion's Eye Diamond is a mana ability (it produces mana, it doesn't target, and it isn't a loyalty ability), so you don't have to wait for it to resolve. LED is special among mana abilities in that it has a timing restriction, but that only limits when you can activate it.
