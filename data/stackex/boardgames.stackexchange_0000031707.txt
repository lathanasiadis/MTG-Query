Q:

Moonlight hunt with trample

If I play Moonlight Hunt, and my Werewolves have trample, does the excess damage from my creatures get applied to the player? 

A:

No, trample only works when dealing combat damage. The damage dealt by creatures because of Moonlight Hunt is non-combat damage.

702.19a Trample is a static ability that modifies the rules for assigning an attacking creature’s combat damage. The ability has no effect when a creature with trample is blocking or is dealing noncombat damage.
