Q:

How much would Spelltwine add to cards that track how many spells were cast?

If I cast Spelltwine and use it to cast two spells from my graveyard and an opponent's, how many spells were cast?

A:

Yes, Spelltwine instructs you to cast those spells, so you do cast them. This counts for things that check for spells being cast or count how many spells were cast, such as the cards linked in the question.
