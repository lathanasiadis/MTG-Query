Q:

Can Rayami, First of the Fallen's ability give it Annihilator or Monstrosity?

If a creature with Annihilator or Monstrosity dies, could Rayami, First of the Fallen obtain those abilities?

A:

No. Rayami, First of the Fallen's ability obtains only the keyword abilities it lists. Any other ability not listed cannot be obtained by that ability. Monstrosity and Annihilator are not listed, so can't be picked up.
In case it helps: the abilities listed on Rayami is the current list of evergreen keyword abilities. (Except Defender.) No set-specific keywords will be found on that list.
