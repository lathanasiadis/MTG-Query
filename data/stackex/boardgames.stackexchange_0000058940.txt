Q:

Are there any MTG cards which test for first strike?

Are there any Magic cards which test for first strike? I’m thinking of something like “Destroy target creature with first strike” or “Creatures with first strike get +1/+1 until end of turn”.

A:

Four cards specifically check for first strike:

Kwende, Pride of Femeref has "Creatures you control with first strike have double strike."
Tresserhorn Skyknight has "Prevent all damage that would be dealt to Tresserhorn Skyknight by creatures with first strike."
Path of Mettle has

When Path of Mettle enters the battlefield, it deals 1 damage to each creature that doesn’t have first strike, double strike, vigilance, or haste.
Whenever you attack with at least two creatures that have first strike, double strike, vigilance, and/or haste, transform Path of Mettle.

Momentum Rumbler has

Whenever Momentum Rumbler attacks, if it doesn’t have first strike, put a first strike counter on it.
Whenever Momentum Rumbler attacks, if it has first strike, it gains double strike until end of turn.

A several cards gain or grant a variety of keyword abilities based on the abilities other creatures or creature cards have, including first strike:

Akroma, Vision of Ixidor
Cairn Wanderer
Concerted Effort
Escaped Shapeshifter
Kathril, Aspect Warper
Majestic Myriarch
Odric, Lunarch Marshal
Thunderous Orator

Several cards exile or otherwise remove cards, and then gain a variety of keyword abilities that those cards have:

Animus of Predation
Death-Mask Duplicant
Eater of Virtue
Rayami, First of the Fallen
Soulflayer
Urborg Scavengers

I found these using the Scryfall searches o:/creature.*with.*first strike/, o:/that.*have.*first strike/, o:/if.*has.*first strike/, and o:/as long as.*has.*first strike/. You could replace "first strike" with other keyword abilities in those searches to find cards that care about those keyword abilities.
