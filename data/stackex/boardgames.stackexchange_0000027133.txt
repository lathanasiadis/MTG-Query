Q:

Creature with equipment attached is returned to owner's hand

If a creature has equipment attached, what happens to the equipment if this creature is returned to the owner's hand?
Does it:

Return to the owner's hand as well?
Go to the graveyard?
Remain on the battlefield?

A:

It will remain on the battlefield.

301.5c ... An Equipment that equips an illegal or nonexistent permanent becomes unattached from that permanent but remains on the battlefield. ...
