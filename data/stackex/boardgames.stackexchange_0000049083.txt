Q:

Does Oko, Thief of Crowns come in a planeswalker deck, or only as an individual card?

I can't find anything about this on Google, Bing or Stack Exchange. Does Oko, Thief of Crowns come with a planeswalker deck, or separately? By separately, I mean in booster packs and stuff like that.

A:

It's 'just' a regular Throne of Eldraine card, found in boosters and tournament packs – if you're lucky, of course; those cards are always random.
There is a preconstructed Throne of Eldraine deck with an Oko planeswalker, but it's Oko, the Trickster. You can find those decks e.g. here.
