Q:

Does a creature with dethrone trigger Kami of Celebration?

I couldn't find anything saying in which order these things happen. So, for example, say I'm attacking my opponent with the highest life total with a couple unmodified creatures while Marchesa, the Black Rose and Kami of Celebration are on the field. Marchesa makes all of these creatures modified, but does that happen in time for it to cause Kami to impulse draw?

A:

No
What happens is you attack, Marchesa triggers. Kami of Celebration would also trigger now, if you were attacking with any modified creatures. But you aren't attacking with any modified creatures. By the time the Marchesa triggers resolve, you are no longer declaring attackers, and it is too late for Kami of Celebration to trigger.
