Q:

MTG: Blocking with multiple units

New MTG player here and this is driving me nuts. How does the following work in magic the gathering?
Scenario 1: A player attacks with a 2/2 creature. The opposing player blocks with 3, 1/1 units. Do all 1/1 units die? Does the 2/2 also die? Do al 3, 1/1 units deal damage?
Scenario 2: A player attacks with a 2/2 creature. The opposing player blocks with 3, 1/2 units. Do all 1/2 units die? Does the 2/2 also die? Do al 3, 1/2 units deal damage?
Scenario 3: A player attacks with a 2/2 creature. The opposing player blocks with 3, 1/3 units. Do the 1/3 units survive? Does the 2/2 also die? Do al 3, 1/3 units deal damage?
Don't think about why you want to do this. I just want to know the rules for this :).

A:

A good thing to remember is that each creature deals total damage equal to its power. When an attacking creature is blocked by more than one creature, it deals its damage distributed as you choose among its blockers (note that it must assign lethal damage to each blocker before assigning damage to the next). Each blocking creature deals its damage to the attacker.
Remember: all damage is dealt at the same time!
To answer your scenarios:
Scenario 1: No more than two* 1/1 blocking creatures will die, and the 2/2 will die (it gets dealt 3 damage, 1 from each).
Scenario 2: Exactly one 1/2 blocker will die, and the 2/2 will also die.
Scenario 3: All 1/3 blockers will survive, because 2 damage is not lethal to any of them. The 2/2 also dies.
*Note that the attacking player may wish to assign all damage to first blocker, killing only one
