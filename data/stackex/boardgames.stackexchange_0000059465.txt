Q:

Would equipping Skullclamp cause Polyraptor to trigger?

Would adding a +1/-1 token to Polyraptor with Skullclamp count as “damage” and trigger Polyraptor? I am guessing the answer is “no”. That this is just an adjustment to toughness, not damage.

A:

You're right, the answer is "no". Giving a creature -1 to toughness can kill an indestructible creature, for example, but any amount of damage cannot kill an indestructible creature. No damage is done; Polyraptor does not trigger.
By the way Skullclamp does not give a "+1/-1 token". It gives a +1/-1 effect. Tokens, like the ones created by Raise the Alarm, are actual permanents that stay on the battlefield until removed. Skullclamp's effect disappears once it's moved to another creature.
