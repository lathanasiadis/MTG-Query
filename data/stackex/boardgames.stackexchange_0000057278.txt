Q:

Young Pyromancer stacking with Village Rites

So i am not too sure how to word this during a paper match. Let's say I have Young Pyromancer already on the battlefield and I cast Village Rites. Would Young Pyromancer's ability go on top of the stack and create the Elemental first which I can then can use as part of the casting cost of Village Rites?

A:

No.
Sacrificing a creature is part of the cost of casting Village Rites:

As an additional cost to cast this spell, sacrifice a creature.

Costs are paid as part of casting the spell and so must all be paid before the spell is cast and Young Pyromancer's ability triggers.
