Q:

Are the gods of Theros considered creatures in my hand?

For example if I have one god of Theros in my hand like Xenagos, God of Revels Can I play it using a Quicksilver Amulet even though I dont have enough devotion?

A:

You can still play it with the Amulet. On Xenagos' page:

The type-changing ability that can make the God not be a creature functions only on the battlefield. It’s always a creature card in other zones, regardless of your devotion to its color.
