What is the full collector number of the MTG card "Aesthetic Consultation" from Unhinged Foil-only Booster card

What is the full collector number of the MTG card "Aesthetic Consultation" from Unhinged Foil-only Booster card?
It reads something like '48/140 Disrobing'. I am particularly curious about the last word.
https://scryfall.com/card/unh/48%E2%98%85/aesthetic-consultation
As well as Farewell to Arms. Is it '56/140 Squizzie'?
https://cards.scryfall.io/large/front/4/7/47b61c3a-5e76-4529-9878-603e49c82743.jpg?1562488372

A:

The collector number of that card is just 48/140. The word "Disrobing" is part of a hidden piece of text in the set with one word on each card. The text can be read by putting the cards in reverse alphabetical order by name, starting with Zzzyxas's Abyss. The text starts with "Here are some more cards that didn't make it." Aesthetic Consultation's name is near the beginning of the alphabet, so its word is near the end of that text. Along with "Ach! Hans, Run!" and _____, it says "Disrobing Scepter (again!)". Similarly, Farewell to Arms, along with Face to Face says "Squizzle Nabizzle".
