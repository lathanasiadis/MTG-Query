Q:

What happens when I deal lethal damage to everyone at the same time?

In an EDH game, I was able to get infinite mana, and I used it to cast a Squall Line with X = 1,000,000. What is the result of this decision? Do I win, or does everyone lose and thus tie?

A:

The relevant rule is this one:

104.4a. If all the players remaining in a game lose simultaneously, the game is a draw.

So the game is a draw. Nobody wins.
